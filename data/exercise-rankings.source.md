# **Muscle Group Exercise Rankings**

This document tracks exercise tier lists, rankings, and key form cues for top-tier movements across different muscle groups. It serves as a comprehensive reference guide for optimizing workout programming, selecting effective movement variations, and executing proper technique for maximum stimulus and mechanical efficiency.

# **Chest Exercises (Jeff Nippard)**

## **Exercise Rankings**

* **S+ Tier (Best of the Best):** Machine Chest Press  
* **S Tier:** Seated Cable PEC Fly  
* **A Tier:** Incline Bench Press, Flat Bench Press, Incline Dumbbell Press, Flat Dumbbell Press, Smith Machine Press, Incline Smith Machine Press, Dips, Deficit Push-Ups, Cable Crossovers, Pec Deck Machine, Dumbbell Flyes (Lengthened Partials), Cable Press-Around, Dumbbell Guillotine Press  
* **B Tier:** Decline Bench Press, Decline Dumbbell Press, Banded Push-Ups  
* **C Tier:** Floor Press, Bodyweight Push-Ups  
* **D Tier:** Dumbbell Pullover, Plyometric Push-Ups  
* **F Tier:** Hex Press, Plate Press (F-Tier Hell), One-Arm Dumbbell Press, Cross-Body Standing Dumbbell Fly, Barbell Guillotine Press (Safety Hazard)

---

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

1. **Machine Chest Press (S+ Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Set the seat height so the handles align with your mid-to-lower chest level. Adjust the starting position to allow a deep stretch at the bottom without putting uncomfortable stress on the shoulder joint.  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Keep your shoulder blades retracted and depressed against the backrest throughout the set. Drive through the handles while focusing on squeezing the pecs together. Advantage: Allows safe, highly stable pushing to failure without needing a spotter.  
2. **Seated Cable PEC Fly (S Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Perform seated with a backrest to eliminate balancing and maximize torso stability. Set the pulleys around shoulder or upper-chest height.  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Keep a slight, fixed bend in your elbows throughout the movement. On the eccentric phase, let the cables stretch your pecs back as far as comfortable. On the concentric phase, focus on bringing your upper arms/biceps together across your chest rather than just touching your hands.  
3. **Flat & Incline Dumbbell Press (A Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Set the bench flat or at a low incline (\~15–30 degrees).  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Lower the dumbbells deep past your ribcage to get a full PEC stretch (a key advantage over barbells). Keep your elbows tucked at roughly 45–60 degrees relative to your torso to protect the shoulders.  
4. **Dips (A Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Lean slightly forward to bias the chest rather than staying completely upright.  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Lower your body under control until you achieve a deep stretch in the pecs at the bottom. Press up smoothly through your palms. Use a dip belt to progressively overload as strength increases.  
5. **Deficit Push-Ups (A Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Place hands on yoga blocks, handles, or weight plates to elevate your hands above the floor.  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Lower your chest past the level of your hands to overcome the depth limitation of standard floor push-ups and get a full PEC stretch. Pause for 2–3 seconds in the bottom stretched position for maximum stimulus.  
6. **Dumbbell Flyes \- Lengthened Partials (A Tier)**  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Focus exclusively on the bottom half of the range of motion where the PEC is under high stretch and mechanical tension. Avoid bringing the dumbbells all the way to the top where tension drops to zero.  
7. **Dumbbell Guillotine Press (A Tier)**  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Flare your elbows out wide and lower dumbbells towards the upper chest/neck area to maximize PEC stretch. Start light to assess shoulder tolerance. (Safer alternative to the barbell version).

# **Back Exercises (Jeff Nippard)**

## **Exercise Rankings**

* **S+ Tier (Best of the Best):** Chest-Supported Row  
* **S Tier:** Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown, Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown, Half-Neeling One-Arm Lat Pulldown, Meadows Row, Cable Row (Close-Grip & Wide-Grip)  
* **A Tier:** Wide-Grip Pull-Up, Neutral-Grip Pull-Up, Cross-Body Lat Pull-Around, Deficit Pendlay Row, One-Arm Dumbbell Row, Kroc Row, Seated/Floor Cable Face Pulls, Cable Lat Pullover (Lat Prayer), Dumbbell Lat Pullover (Lengthened Partials)  
* **B Tier:** Chin-Up, Barbell Row, Pendlay Row, T-Bar Row (Freestanding), Standard Rope Face Pull  
* **C Tier:** Deadlift, Yates Row, Inverted Row  
* **D Tier:** Rack Pull (Standard)  
* **F Tier:** Renegade Rows (F-Tier Hell), Combo Rows (Row \+ Curl / Press / Kickback), Above-the-Knee Rack Pull (Equipment Hazard / Ego Lifting)

---

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

8. **Chest-Supported Row (S+ Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Set up on a chest-supported row machine or brace your chest firmly against an incline bench (e.g., Helms Row).  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Eliminates momentum and lower back fatigue. Drive your elbows back past your torso to hit both the lats and upper/mid back with maximum stability. Focus on pulling through the elbows rather than yanking with the hands.  
9. **Wide-Grip & Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown (S Tier)**  
   * **Form & Setup:** Sit down and secure your thighs firmly under the pads to ensure stability.  
   * **Execution & Cues:** Lean back slightly (10–15 degrees). Pull the bar down toward your upper chest, focusing on pulling your elbows down into your sides. Neutral grip offers slightly higher lat bias, while wide grip engages mid-traps and upper back alongside the lats.  
10. **Half-Neeling One-Arm Lat Pulldown (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Kneel on one knee facing a single cable stack. Brace against your front knee or an incline bench for stability.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Pull the cable unilaterally, driving the elbow straight down into your hip. Great for isolating each lat independently and fixing side-to-side muscular imbalances.  
11. **Meadows Row (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set up at a landmine station or barbell corner, standing perpendicular to the bar. Stagger your stance and brace your opposite arm firmly against your front thigh/knee.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Grip the fat end of the barbell sleeve. Drive your elbow up and out to smash the upper/mid-back and traps with high tension and a deep bottom stretch.  
12. **Cable Rows \- Close-Grip & Wide-Grip (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Sit tall on the cable row bench with feet planted firmly.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lean forward at the hips during the eccentric phase to achieve a deep stretch in the lats. Pull your elbows back to your sides, driving thoracic extension as you complete the concentric contraction.  
13. **Deficit Pendlay Row (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Stand on bumper plates or a low platform over the barbell. Bend over until your torso is parallel to the floor.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** The deficit allows a deeper stretch off the floor. Explosively pull the bar to your lower sternum/upper abdomen, then lower under control to a complete dead stop.  
14. **Cable Lat Pullover / Lat Prayer (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Use a rope or straight bar on a high pulley. Step back and hinge forward at the hips.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Keep a slight fixed bend in your elbows. Sweep your hands downward in an arc towards your thighs by driving strictly with your elbows. Incline your torso forward at the top to maximize the overhead lat stretch.

# **Glute Exercises (Jeff Nippard)**

## **Exercise Rankings**

* **S+ Tier (Best of the Best):** Walking Lunge  
* **S Tier:** Machine Hip Abduction (Upper Glute \#1 Choice), Deficit/Front-Foot-Elevated Lunge, 45-Degree Back Extension  
* **A Tier:** Machine Hip Thrust / Glute Drive (Mid Glute \#1 Choice), Single-Leg Dumbbell Hip Thrust, Romanian Deadlift (Lower Glute \#1 Choice), Barbell Squat, Smith Machine Squat, Bulgarian Split Squat, Smith Machine Lunge, Cable Glute Kickbacks, Step-Ups  
* **B Tier:** Barbell Hip Thrust, Glute Bridge, Cable Hip Abduction, Curtsy Lunge, Conventional Deadlift, Sumo Deadlift, Cable Pull-Through  
* **C Tier:** Frog Pumps, Lateral Banded Walk  
* **D Tier:** Donkey Kicks (F-Tier Hell / Worst of the Worst), Fire Hydrants, Kettlebell Swings

---

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

15. **Walking Lunge (S+ Tier \- Overall Best Glute Exercise)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides or a barbell across your back. Take long strides forward.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lean your torso slightly forward (\~30 degrees) as you descend into each lunge to stretch the glutes deeply. Drive up and forward through your front heel/hips rather than pushing solely with your quads.  
16. **Machine Hip Abduction (S Tier \- Upper Glute \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Sit on the machine and add a foam pad inside the leg pads if extra range of motion is desired.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lean your torso forward about 30 degrees to align the resistance with upper glute (glute medius/minimus) fibers. Press outward under control and control the eccentric return for a deep stretch. High reps (15–20) or heavier loads work well.  
17. **Front-Foot-Elevated / Deficit Lunge (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Elevate your front foot on a small platform or box (2–4 inches).  
    * **Execution & Cues:** The deficit provides extra depth and stretch at the bottom of the movement. Keep a slight forward torso lean (\~30 degrees) and drive through the hip.  
18. **45-Degree Back Extension (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set the pad height just below your hip crease to allow full hip flexion.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Round your upper back slightly (thoracic flexion) and turn your toes slightly outward to reduce spinal erector engagement and isolate the glutes. Extend at the hips to pull yourself up, squeezing the glutes hard at top lockout.  
19. **Machine Hip Thrust / Glute Drive (A Tier \- Mid Glute \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Strap the belt comfortably across your hips below the iliac spine.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Superior to barbell hip thrusts due to setup ease, comfort, and stability. Drive hips straight up, locking out at the top while keeping chin tucked and ribs down to maximize mid-glute tension.  
20. **Romanian Deadlift (A Tier \- Lower Glute \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Hold a barbell or dumbbells with a shoulder-width stance.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Push your hips back as far as possible (hip hinge) with a slight knee bend until you feel a deep stretch in your lower glutes and hamstrings. Drive your hips forward to lockout.  
21. **Bulgarian Split Squat & Step-Ups (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** For split squats, place front foot far enough forward to allow a deep hip stretch. For step-ups, set box height at or slightly above knee level and hold a handle/rack for stability.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lean torso forward slightly and focus on driving through the lead hip rather than extending purely through the front knee.

# **Biceps Exercises (Jeff Nippard)**

## **Exercise Rankings**

* **S+ Tier (Best of the Best):** Face-Away Bayesian Cable Curl  
* **S Tier:** 45-Degree Preacher Curl (Dumbbell or EZ-Bar), Machine Preacher Curl, Hammer Grip Preacher Curl  
* **A Tier:** EZ-Bar Curl, Standing Dumbbell Curl (Simultaneous), Incline Dumbbell Curl, Lying Dumbbell Curl, Cable Curl (Standard), Face-Away Behind-the-Back Cable Curl Variation, Modified 21s (Short \-\> Full \-\> Long), Cheat Curl, Strict Curl, Dumbbell Hammer Curl, Inverse Zottman Curl  
* **B Tier:** Barbell Curl, Flat Bench Stretch-Focused Curl, Chin-Ups  
* **C Tier:** Scott Curl (Vertical Preacher), Drag Curl, Spider Curl, Old-School 21s  
* **D Tier:** None  
* **F Tier:** Waiter Curl (F-Tier Hell / Worst of the Worst)

---

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

22. **Face-Away Bayesian Cable Curl (S+ Tier \- Overall Best Biceps Exercise)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set the cable pulley around hand/waist height. Face away from the cable stack, take 1–2 steps forward so your arms are positioned behind your torso, placing the long head of the bicep into a maximum pre-stretch.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Curl both arms simultaneously while keeping your elbows set back behind your body. Drive through your pinkies to emphasize supination. Excellent movement for building the biceps peak.  
23. **45-Degree Preacher Curl \- Dumbbell / EZ-Bar / Machine (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Adjust the preacher bench to a standard \~45-degree angle. Ensure your armpits sit comfortably against the top pad with upper arms fully flush.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lower the weight all the way down to full elbow extension to capitalize on high tension in the stretched position. Lock elbows in place to prevent momentum or shoulder involvement.  
24. **Hammer Grip Preacher Curl (S Tier \- Top Choice for Brachialis & Biceps Peak)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Position upper arms on a 45-degree preacher bench holding dumbbells with a neutral (palms facing inward, thumbs pointing up) grip.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lower under control to a complete stretch. Taking away supination shifts high tension to the underlying brachialis, which pushes the biceps up to accentuate the peak.  
25. **EZ-Bar Curl & Standing Dumbbell Curl (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Use a shoulder-width grip on the outer curves of the EZ-bar to reduce wrist strain. For dumbbells, perform both arms simultaneously rather than alternating to maintain constant muscle tension.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Focus on supination by driving through your pinkies as you curl upward. Keep elbows relatively fixed at your sides to prevent shoulder takeover.  
26. **Inverse Zottman Curl (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Stand tall holding dumbbells.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Curl the weight up using a neutral hammer grip (stronger position engaging brachialis), then rotate your palms fully upward (supinated) at the top and lower the weight under control during the eccentric phase.  
27. **Modified 21s (A Tier)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Perform in reverse order of traditional 21s to match the anabolic stimulus (Short \-\> Full \-\> Long): start with 7 reps in the shortened top-half (e.g. seated), 7 full-range-of-motion reps, and finish with 7+ lengthened bottom-half partial reps to failure.

## **Rear Delts Tier List (Shorts Edition)**

**Rankings:**

* **S Tier:** Reverse Pec Deck (Sideways Setup)  
* **High A Tier:** Lying Dumbbell Reverse Fly, Reverse Cable Crossover  
* **A Tier:** Rope Face Pulls (Underhand Grip)  
* **Low B Tier:** Wide-Grip Rows, Reverse Pec Deck (Standard Facing Forward)  
* **C Tier:** Standing Dumbbell Reverse Fly

**Key Execution Cues:**

28. **Reverse Pec Deck (Sideways Setup \- S Tier):** Sit sideways on the machine so your arm sweeps across your chest. This doubles the range of motion compared to facing forward and provides a deep rear delt stretch under even tension.  
29. **Lying Dumbbell Reverse Fly (High A Tier):** Perform lying down on a bench to maintain tension on the rear delt at the bottom stretch position (unlike standing dumbbell reverse flies).  
30. **Reverse Cable Crossover (High A Tier):** Cross arms in front of body; when arms cross over at the top, it provides a deep stretch with high cable tension.  
31. **Rope Face Pulls \- Underhand Grip (A Tier):** Use an underhand grip to bias the rear delts over the upper traps.  
32. **Wide-Grip Rows (Low B Tier):** Solid for overloading rear delts with heavier weights, though mid-traps will partially take over.  
33. **Standing Dumbbell Reverse Fly (C Tier):** Suffers from zero tension at the bottom and a lack of stretch in the starting position.

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

34. **Cable Lateral Raise (S+ Tier \- Side Delt \#1 Choice & Overall Best Shoulder Exercise)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set cable pulley at around hand height to achieve maximum tension (90-degree angle between cable and forearm) when the side delt is in its most stretched position. Optionally use wrist cuffs (or hold a lacrosse ball inside the cuff) to take forearm/grip fatigue out of the equation.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Raise the cable out and away from your body under control. Provides smooth, even tension across the entire range of motion.  
35. **Behind-the-Back Cuffed Cable Lateral Raise (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Hook wrists into cuffs on a dual cable stack, step over one cable, turn around, cross cables behind your back, step forward, and perform raises.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Raises both arms simultaneously for double the time efficiency while maintaining optimal stretched tension on side delts.  
36. **Reverse Pec Deck \- Sideways Setup (S Tier \- Rear Delt \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Sit sideways on a reverse pec deck machine to allow one arm at a time to cross further across your chest.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Sweeping one arm across your chest achieves a much deeper stretch on the rear delts than facing forward. Drive back through the elbow while keeping chest firmly braced.  
37. **Reverse Cable Crossover (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set pulleys high, cross your arms in front of your chest holding the cables directly (without handles). Alternate which arm crosses on top from set to set.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Focus on sweeping the cables out and back rather than yanking them. Provides an enormous range of motion with deep stretch and smooth tension across the rear delts.  
38. **Machine Shoulder Press (A Tier \- Front Delt \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Choose a machine with comfortable neutral or semi-slanted handles that allows a deep overhead stretch at the bottom.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Offers high stability compared to barbell OHP, allowing you to drive front delts straight to complete failure safely.  
39. **Lean-In Dumbbell Lateral Raise (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Hold a dumbbell in one hand while leaning your torso into a bench, rack, or pole with the non-working arm.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Leaning in shifts the resistance curve, placing significantly higher tension on the side delt at the bottom stretched position compared to standard standing raises.  
40. **Arnold-Style Side-Lying Dumbbell Raise (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Lie sideways on an incline bench holding a light dumbbell.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Raises the dumbbell across your body with high stretch-position tension and deep range of motion, providing a massive side-delt pump.

* **B Tier:** Cable Triceps Press Down (Rope), Dumbbell French Press, JM Press (Barbell), Close Grip Dips, Machine Dips, Diamond Push-Ups

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

41. **Overhead Cable Triceps Extension \- Bar (S+ Tier \- Overall Best Triceps Exercise)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set cable stack to high or mid height, turn away from the cable, and extend arms overhead.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Moving the arms overhead puts the long head of the triceps into a deep stretch under high cable tension. Keep elbows tucked near ears and extend through the forearms under control. Shown in studies to produce \~40% more triceps growth compared to standard pushdowns.  
42. **EZ-Bar SkullCrushers (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Lie flat on a bench using an EZ-bar to reduce wrist strain. Start the range of motion with your arms angled slightly back behind your head rather than straight up vertical to maintain constant tension at the top.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Arc the bar back behind your head on the eccentric phase to get a deep stretch at the bottom rather than stopping literally at your forehead. Drive forearms back up smoothly.  
43. **Cable Triceps Press Down \- Straight/EZ-Bar (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Attach a straight or EZ-bar to a high pulley. Stand tall and pin elbows to sides.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Provides higher rigid stability and overload potential than a rope. Drive the bar down to full elbow extension to maximize stimulus on the medial and lateral heads.  
44. **Katana Triceps Extension (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set dual cable pulleys around waist height. Cross arms overhead, grabbing the opposite cable, and step forward into the scapular plane (arms angled 30–40° forward).  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Extends each arm individually in a very natural, comfortable line of pull while hitting the long head in a deep stretch.  
45. **Cable Triceps Kickbacks (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set cable pulley at chest or waist height. Hold cable directly or with a handle and step back.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Drives the arm back behind the torso, allowing the long head to reach its fully contracted/shortened peak squeeze position. Maintain smooth cable tension throughout.  
46. **Close Grip Bench Press (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Lie on a flat bench using a shoulder-width grip on the barbell.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Excellent compound movement for massive progressive overload. Keep elbows tucked closer to your ribcage to heavily bias the lateral and medial heads.

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S+, S, and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

47. **Hack Squat (S+ Tier \- Overall Best Quad Exercise)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set feet lower on the platform to maximize knee flexion and knee forward displacement. If the machine bottoms out early, place yoga blocks or pads behind your shoulders/upper back to achieve a full, deep stretch.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lower under control as deep as hip and knee mobility allow. Keep back flat against the pad and drive up through the entire foot. Eliminates balance limitations to direct maximal tension straight to the quads.  
48. **Barbell High Bar Back Squat (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Place bar high on the upper traps. Set stance slightly wider than hip-width with toes turned slightly outward.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Keep chest upright and bend knees deeply to allow the knees to track forward over toes, breaking parallel for maximum quad stretch. Drive straight up through the mid-foot.  
49. **Smith Machine Squat (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set feet slightly forward of the bar path to maintain an upright torso throughout the descent.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Provides high stability so you can safely push sets directly to absolute muscular failure without fear of losing balance or needing a spotter.  
50. **Bulgarian Split Squat (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Place rear foot on a bench and take a slightly shorter stance than you would for glute bias to force greater knee extension.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lower single leg deep into knee flexion until calf meets thigh. Drives intense quad tension and fixes side-to-side muscle imbalances.  
51. **Seated Leg Extension \- Lean-Back Setup (A Tier \- Essential for Rectus Femoris)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Set the backrest reclined back rather than completely upright.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Reclining the seat back places the long rectus femoris head into a deeper stretch at the hip, maximizing growth across all 4 quad heads. Pause briefly at full lockout and control the negative.  
52. **Reverse Nordics (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Kneel on a padded mat with feet unflattened or flat on the floor.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Maintain a straight line from knees through hips to shoulders. Lean backward smoothly by hinging purely at the knees to place an extreme lengthened stretch on the quads before driving back up.

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

53. **Dumbbell Wrist Curls (S Tier \- Anterior Forearm \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Simple, basic, and tried-and-true muscle builder for isolated anterior forearm growth. Curl wrists upward with forearms supported.  
54. **Dumbbell Wrist Extensions (S Tier \- Posterior Forearm \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Simple and highly effective for building the back (extensors) of the forearm. Extend wrists upward while keeping forearms braced on a bench or knees.  
55. **Preacher Hammer Curls (S Tier \- Brachioradialis \#1 Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Perform hammer curls using a neutral grip on a preacher bench.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Increases torso and upper arm stability while placing higher mechanical tension directly onto the brachioradialis compared to standard standing hammer curls.  
56. **Reverse Grip Curls (High A Tier)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Awesome option for building up the back of the forearms. Maintain an overhand (pronated) grip throughout the curl. Note that biceps and brachialis still assist to an extent.  
57. **Plate Pinches (Low A Tier)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Pinch smooth weight plates between your fingers and thumb to target pinch grip strength and forearms. Easy to progressively overload by extending hold time weekly.

## **Form & Cue Notes for Top Tier Exercises (S and Key A-Tier Alternatives)**

58. **Reverse Crunch with AbMat (S Tier \- Top Lower Abs Choice for Beginners & Advanced)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Lie on a flat bench placing an AbMat or rolled towel under your lower back to allow your spine to extend fully for a deep stretch at the bottom.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Keep knees bent at 90 degrees. Focus on pushing your tailbone up and curling your pelvis inward toward your ribcage (forming a C-shape) rather than just pulling knees to chest. Control the eccentric on the way down. Extend legs outward to increase difficulty.  
59. **Garhammer Raise (S Tier \- Top Lower Abs Choice for Advanced)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Lie on a flat bench with your hips positioned right off the end edge.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Tuck knees in and lower legs as far down as possible without letting feet touch the floor to achieve a deep stretch under tension. Curl your pelvis upward smoothly without using momentum or swaying your legs.  
60. **Face-Away Lat Pulldown / Cable Crunch (S Tier \- Top Upper Abs Choice)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Position yourself facing AWAY from the cable stack on a kneeling, standing, or seated lat pulldown station. Adjust the backrest/pad to lock your hips firmly in place.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Facing away creates maximum tension at the top stretch position. Focus on flexing your spine by bringing your ribcage down toward your pelvis into a tight C-shape rather than hinging at the hips.  
61. **Assisted Lat Pulldown Crunch (S Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Sit on a lat pulldown machine (or assisted pull-up setup) where the cable provides a light upward pull.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** The upward assistance allows you to control the full eccentric stretch at the top and execute a full spinal crunch without form breakdown. Excellent for beginners to master deep-stretch crunches safely.  
62. **Garhammer Hold (A Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Hang hips off the end of a bench and tuck knees into chest.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Lower your legs to the point of maximum stretched tension and hold isometrically. High lower-abs activation in the stretched position.  
63. **Captain's Chair & Hanging Leg Raise with C-Shape Curl (A/B Tier)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Support upper body on forearm pads (Captain's Chair) or hang from straps/bar.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Do not simply lift your legs with your hip flexors. Focus on actively curling your pelvis upward to round the lower spine into a C-shape so the lower abs initiate the pull.

## **Form & Execution Cues for Highlighted Movements**

64. **Deficit Push-Ups (S Tier \- Chest)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Elevate hands on blocks or handles to allow the chest to drop below hand level for a full lengthened stretch. As you press up, body angle becomes more perpendicular to gravity, creating an optimal lengthened resistance curve.  
65. **Cable Overhead Triceps Extension with Bar (S Tier \- Triceps)**  
    * **Form & Setup:** Attach a bar (not a rope) to a cable stack.  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Avoid using rope attachments, as spreading ropes wastes energy at lockout and forces load reductions at the crucial bottom stretch. Pause 1–2 seconds in the bottom stretched position.  
66. **Prime Machine Row (God Tier \- Back)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Use chest support to eliminate lower back fatigue during upper/mid-back rowing. Place weight on the top pin to maximize mechanical tension at full stretch.  
67. **Dumbbell / Cable Pullover (S Tier \- Lats & Triceps Long Head)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Arc the load back over your head to place lats and long head of the triceps under peak stretch tension without fatiguing your forearms/grip.  
68. **Advanced Barbell Bent-Over Row (A Tier \- Whole Back)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Start in a deep stretch. As you pull up, leverage your hips up slightly to adjust leverage dynamically, increasing mechanical advantage at lockout while keeping maximum stretch tension at the bottom.  
69. **Barbell Front Raises \- Pinkies Up (B Tier \- Side Delt Bias)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Perform with a pronated grip pointing pinkies slightly upward (rather than thumbs up) to engage side delt fibers. Lower slowly through the eccentric phase under control.  
70. **Seated Leg Extension Adjustment (C to S Tier \- Quads)**  
    * **Execution & Cues:** Recline the backrest backward as far as possible (extending hips from 90° to \~40°) to stretch the rectus femoris and dramatically boost quad growth.
